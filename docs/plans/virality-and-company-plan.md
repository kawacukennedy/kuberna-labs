## 6. Ads — when, where, and how (don't burn money early)

Your notes' guru-pyramid section is a warning: ads that amplify nothing are tuition. The rule: **never buy ads for unproven content.**

- **Until you have a winner: $0 on ads.** Distribution is free in the communities above.
- **Gate to turn on ads:** one organic post hits **100+ quality engagements** (comments/quote-tweets, not likes). That post is now a *proven wedge* — copy it into an ad.
- **First ad test:** boost the proven post on **X, $100/day for 3 days**, targeting: AI agents, crypto/web3, "build in public", agent infrastructure. Landing page = **the signal form**, not the repo.
- **Kill/bypass by signal cost:** if cost-per-signal > **$15**, kill and move on. If < $8, double down to $300/day.
- **TikTok/Shorts:** only the 3 AM and Math.sin story beats; if the same story flatlines organically, skip paid on that platform.
- **Reinvest:** revenue (grants + pilots) funds the next ad round — the ads become a compounding loop once the company has income (the snowball in the scale notes).
