## Recommended Action Plan

### Phase 1: Foundation (Week 1-2)
1. **Stripe Atlas** — Incorporate Delaware C-corp ($500)
   - Unlocks corporate email (you@company.com)
   - Unlocks US-based programs requiring corporate email
   - $2,500 Stripe credits + $50K+ partner perks included

### Phase 2: High-Priority Applications (Week 2-4)
2. **Microsoft Founders Hub** — Apply immediately (bootstrapped eligible)
3. **Google Cloud for Startups** — Apply for $350K credits
4. **AWS Activate Founders Track** — Apply for $1K credits (no VC needed)
5. **Alchemy for Startups** — Apply for Web3-specific credits
6. **MongoDB Atlas for Startups** — Apply for database credits
7. **Pulumi for Startups** — Apply for infrastructure-as-code credits

### Phase 3: Join Accelerator (Month 2-3)
- Apply to Y Combinator, Techstars, or similar accelerator
- This unlocks: DigitalOcean Hatch, Cloudflare v2, Datadog, AWS Portfolio Track

### Phase 4: Medium-Priority Applications (Month 3+)
- Apply to remaining programs once accelerator accepted

## Key Insights

1. **Stripe Atlas is the foundational step** — Incorporating a Delaware C-corp unlocks corporate email and US-based programs. Most programs require corporate email or VC/accelerator referral.

2. **Accelerator is the unlock** — Programs like DigitalOcean Hatch, Cloudflare v2, Datadog, and AWS Portfolio Track require accelerator enrollment. Joining Y Combinator, Techstars, or similar unlocks $500K+ in additional credits.

3. **Total potential value**: $1M+ in cloud credits and services if you incorporate (Stripe Atlas), join an accelerator, and apply to all programs.

4. **Web3-specific programs**: Alchemy, QuickNode, The Graph are directly relevant to your agent orchestration platform. Apply early.

5. **No geographic restrictions**: Most programs accept global startups. Rwanda-based is not a barrier.

6. **Bootstrapped founder advantage**: Microsoft, AWS Founders Track, MongoDB, and Pulumi accept bootstrapped founders without VC backing.
